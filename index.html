HOW TO PLAY
1/6
🔵
Pop the Bubbles!
Tap blue bubbles to pop them. Each pop earns points and credits. Miss one — lose a life. You start with 3 lives.
☠️
Avoid the Toxic!
Green bubbles with ☠ are deadly. Touch one = instant game over. Stay away!
🥉🥈🥇
Special Bubbles
Bronze (15s), Silver (30s) and Gold (60s) fly diagonally — pop them for bonus credits! ❤ heart bubbles restore a life (max 5).
🔥
Combo System
Pop bubbles within 1 second of each other to build a combo (max x3). Higher combo = more points and more credits per pop!
x1 = 5pts +1💰
x2 = 10pts +2💰
x3 = 15pts +3💰
⚗️
Power-Ups
Buy power-ups in Extras → Powers. Use them mid-game with the buttons on the right side of the screen.
❄️ Freeze 3s
🐢 Slow 10s
🧲 Magnet 10s
🛍️
Shop & Daily
Spend credits in the Shop to unlock 100 bubble skins, 20 toxic skins, and 15 backgrounds. Log in daily for bonus credits and spin the Wheel of Fortune!
